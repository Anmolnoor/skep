# Company research

Tools: search_web, read_url, start_research, delegate_analysis

1. `search_web` the company + "what does <X> do"; read the company's
   own about/product page AND one independent source (`read_url` each,
   or `start_research` when it's a real dossier) — the company's page
   is marketing; label it as such.
2. Structure: what they sell, to whom, business model, size/funding
   (with the date of the number — funding data staling fast), top 3
   competitors and the one-line difference.
3. "Should we partner/buy/worry" asks → `delegate_analysis` with a
   bull and a bear lens, then synthesize with your own call.
4. Every number gets a source and a date; anything from memory is
   labeled "unverified, as of training" — never dressed as a fetched
   fact.
